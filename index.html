Jose Arellano Martorellet

Welcome! I am a PhD Candidate in Behavioral Decision Research in the Department of Social and Decision Sciences at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Gretchen B. Chapman and Silvia Saccardo.

My research examines how people engage with information, form judgments, and make decisions, particularly in health. I use field experiments, online experiments, and administrative data to study how communication and behavioral interventions shape what people do.

Before my doctoral studies, I worked extensively on applied research and behavioral interventions with public and private organizations in Peru and across Latin America.
