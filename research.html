A large-scale randomized trial with UCLA Health testing whether behaviorally informed patient communications increase engagement with, and completion of, recommended preventive care.

Selective (In)attention: Evidence from Healthcare

Field and online experiments on how the presentation of health information affects patients’ willingness to engage with healthcare communications, including cases where interventions discourage rather than encourage engagement.

Jose Arellano Martorellet, Gretchen B. Chapman, and Silvia Saccardo

Experiments on how observable cues, such as credentials, technical language, appearance, and message plausibility, shape perceptions of expertise, credibility, and trust in online health communication.
